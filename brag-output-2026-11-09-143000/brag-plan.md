# Brag Plan: InhaleSkill AI

## Project Essence
**InhaleSkill AI** — Universal Offline AI Learning & Productivity Assistant. Premium Blue & White Liquid Glass UI. 100% local inference via llama.cpp. No cloud, no login, no telemetry. Android, Linux, Windows, macOS.

## Tone: Polished
Serious, elegant, premium. Clean motion. Trust-building. The app speaks for itself.

## Format
Landscape (1920×1080), 20 seconds

## Music Cue Guidance
Track: *happy-beats-business-moves-vol-1* (bundled, upbeat corporate)
- 0:00–0:02.5 — Intro pad, subtle arp (hook)
- 0:02.5–0:06 — Kick enters, bass groove (reveal)
- 0:06–0:10 — Syncopated lead, energy builds (inference demo)
- 0:10–0:14 — Breakdown, lighter texture (school/college)
- 0:14–0:17.5 — Full groove returns (UI montage)
- 0:17.5–0:20 — Outro chord, decay (punchline)

Beat sync: cuts on downbeats at 2.5s, 6s, 10s, 14s, 17.5s.
